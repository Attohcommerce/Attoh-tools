SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This page explains how we get your order to you: where we deliver, what it costs, how long it takes and what to do if something goes wrong along the way.

Delivery area

We currently deliver to addresses in Canada only. If your address is a PO box or in a remote community, email us before you order so we can confirm that our carriers can reach you.

What delivery costs

Nothing. Every order ships free, whatever you spend and however many items you add. There are no handling fees at checkout.

Order cut-off and handling

Orders placed on a business day (Monday to Friday, excluding Canadian public holidays) before 6:00 PM Eastern Time are picked up the same day. Orders placed after the cut-off, on weekends or on public holidays enter the queue on the next business day.

Handling takes 1 to 2 business days. During peak periods such as Black Friday, the holiday season and Boxing Week it can take a little longer; if your order is delayed by more than two business days we will email you.

How long delivery takes

After your parcel leaves us, transit within Canada takes 4 to 8 business days. Counting handling and transit together, you can expect your order within 5 to 10 business days of placing it. These are typical timeframes, not guarantees: winter weather, carrier volume and remote locations can add a few days.

Tracking your parcel

You will receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking link as soon as your order is on its way. Tracking can take 24 to 48 hours to show its first scan. If there has been no update for more than five business days, email us and we will chase the carrier for you.

Getting the address right

Please double-check your address, including the unit number and postal code, before you pay. You can correct it by emailing us within 30 minutes of placing the order. Once the parcel has been handed to the carrier we can no longer redirect it. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or incomplete, we will contact you to arrange a new delivery; a new shipping charge may apply in that case.

Parcels that go missing or arrive damaged

If tracking says your parcel was delivered but you do not have it, check with household members, neighbours and your building's mailroom or community mailbox first, then contact us within 30 days of the delivery date. If your order arrives damaged, send us a few photos of the item and the packaging within 30 days of delivery. In both cases we open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund your money if the parcel cannot be recovered.

Several items, several parcels

Occasionally the items in one order are dispatched separately. You will receive a tracking link for each parcel, and you never pay extra for split shipments.

Returning something

Sending an item back works differently from delivery. Everything you need to know is in our Return & Refund Policy.

RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Not quite right? You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to let us know you want to send something back. Below you will find who pays for what, how the process works and when you get your money.

The 30-day window

A return request must reach us within 30 days of the delivery date shown in your tracking. Requests that arrive later cannot be accepted for change-of-mind returns.

What we can take back

Items need to come back the way they arrived: unworn, unwashed, undamaged, with every label and tag still attached and, where possible, in the original packaging. Please include your order number so we can match the parcel to you.

We cannot accept items that have been worn, washed or altered, items with a removed hygiene seal, or pieces that were made or personalised to order.

Starting a return in three steps

- Email info@consistentcouture.com or use the contact form. Mention your order number, which item(s) you are returning and why.
- Within one business day we confirm your return and send you the return address together with simple packing instructions.
- Send the parcel within 14 days of our confirmation and keep your proof of postage. Parcels sent before we have confirmed the return, or to a different address, may not be accepted.

Who pays the return shipping

- You changed your mind, ordered the wrong size or the item is not what you hoped for: you pay the return postage. Choose a tracked service; we cannot refund parcels that never reach us.
- The item is faulty, damaged, wrong or not as described: we pay. Email us photos within 30 days of delivery and we will arrange everything, including a prepaid label or a reimbursement of your postage.

We do not charge restocking, handling or processing fees of any kind.

Refunds

When your parcel arrives we check the items and email you a confirmation. Approved refunds are sent to the payment method you used at checkout within 10 business days of that confirmation. Depending on your bank or card issuer it can take a few more days before the amount shows on your statement. If you would rather have store credit, just say so and we will email you a credit for the full amount, valid on anything in the store.

Exchanges

We do not hold items while a return travels back to us. For a different size or colour, return the original item and place a new order for the one you want; that way you get it fastest. If you need an exchange because we sent the wrong item, we send the correct one at our cost.

Returning part of a discounted order

If a discount code or a multi-buy offer (Buy 2, Buy 3, Buy 4+) applied to your order and you return only some items, we recalculate the discount on the items you keep. Your refund is the amount you paid minus the discount you would have received on the remaining items.

Changing or cancelling an order

You can cancel or change an order free of charge within 30 minutes of placing it. Email us right away with your order number. After 30 minutes the order is already in handling and can no longer be cancelled, but you can of course return it once it arrives.

Your legal rights

Nothing on this page limits the rights you have under the consumer protection laws of the province or territory where you live.
