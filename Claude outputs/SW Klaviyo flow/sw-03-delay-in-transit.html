Before you ask

01

Is my order lost?

No. Your parcel is in the carrier’s network and on its way — it’s just moving slower than it should.

02

What caused the delay?

Delays like this usually happen at a sorting hub or during busy periods. It sits with the carrier, not with you or your order.

03

Do I need to contact the carrier?

No need. We’ve already contacted our shipping partner and are following it up for you.

04

My tracking hasn’t changed?

That’s expected during a hold-up. Tracking updates again once the parcel reaches its next scan point.
