SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: [TBA – date of publishing]

1. Where we ship
Clove & Cloth ships to addresses within the United States. We are not able to deliver to PO boxes, military addresses (APO, FPO and DPO) or areas our carriers do not serve. A small number of items may be limited by US regulations or carrier rules; if that affects your order, we will contact you before it ships.

2. Shipping cost
Shipping is free on every order. There is no minimum spend and no hidden handling fee at checkout.

3. Processing time
We prepare and hand over orders within 1 to 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed before 6:00 PM Central Time on a business day start processing the same day; orders placed later, or at the weekend, start on the next business day. In busy periods such as holiday sales, processing can take a little longer.

4. Delivery time
Once your order has left us, delivery takes 4 to 8 business days. In total, most orders arrive within 5 to 10 business days of being placed. These are estimates: carrier delays and severe weather can occasionally add time.

5. Tracking
As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also check the status at any time on our Track your order page. If your tracking has not updated for several days, email us and we will look into it with the carrier.

6. Address details
Please check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us within 30 minutes of placing your order and we will correct it. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to arrange a new shipment; an additional shipping charge may apply.

7. Lost or damaged parcels
If your parcel is marked as delivered but has not reached you, or it arrives damaged, email info@cloveandcloth.com with your order number (and photos for damaged items) within 30 days of the expected delivery date. We will open an investigation with the carrier and arrange a replacement or refund where the parcel cannot be found or the item cannot be used.

8. Returns
Free shipping covers delivery to you. For returns, please see our Return & Refund Policy.

9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time, for example to improve our service or to meet platform requirements. The version published at the time you placed your order applies to that order.

RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Last updated: [TBA – date of publishing]

1. 30 days to return
You can request a return within 30 days of the day your order is delivered. After 30 days we are unable to accept change-of-mind returns.

2. Condition of returned items
To qualify for a return, items must be:
- unworn, unwashed and unused;
- in their original condition, with all original tags still attached;
- sent back in their original packaging where possible, together with your order number as proof of purchase.

3. Items we cannot accept
For hygiene and quality reasons we cannot accept items with a broken hygiene seal, personalised or made-to-order pieces, or items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.

4. How to start a return
1. Email info@cloveandcloth.com (or use our Contact page) with your order number, the item(s) you want to return and the reason.
2. We review your request and reply with the return address and clear instructions.
3. Please wait for our approval before sending anything back. Parcels sent without approval may not be accepted.

5. Return shipping costs
- Change of mind (wrong size ordered, style not as expected, no longer needed): return shipping is paid by you. We recommend a tracked service, as we cannot refund parcels that do not reach us.
- Faulty, damaged, incorrect or not-as-described items: we cover the full cost of the return.
We never charge a restocking or handling fee.

6. Faulty, damaged or incorrect items
If something arrives faulty, damaged, incorrect or not as described, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a few photos. We will arrange a replacement, repair or full refund at no cost to you. This does not affect your rights under applicable consumer protection laws.

7. Refunds
Once your return arrives and has been checked, we email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card provider may need a few extra days to show the amount on your statement. If you prefer, you can choose store credit instead; we send it by email and it can be used on anything in our store.

8. Exchanges
The quickest way to get a different size or color is to return the original item following the steps above and place a new order for the item you want. If your exchange is needed because of our mistake, we will send the correct item at no extra cost.

9. Discounts and partial returns
If your order used a discount, promotion or multi-buy offer and you return only part of it, the discount is recalculated on the items you keep. The refund is the amount you paid minus the adjusted discount.

10. Cancellations
You can cancel or change your order within 30 minutes of placing it by emailing us straight away with your order number. After that, your order is already being prepared and the standard return process applies once it arrives.

11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The version published at the time you placed your order applies to that order.
